FMCSA-derived product additions:

- Track Clearinghouse designation and authorized functions: queries, violation reporting, RTD reporting.
- Expose RTD/follow-up tracking.
- Preserve consortium pool/member visibility and owner-operator enrollment context.
- Keep employer responsibility visible: a C/TPA assists with delegated functions but does not replace the employer's regulatory responsibility.

Important implementation boundary:
- This portal records and manages screenings4u workflows. It does not itself register a C/TPA in the federal Clearinghouse or create a federal designation. Those federal actions occur in the FMCSA Clearinghouse.
